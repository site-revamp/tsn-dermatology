Senior Consultant Dermatologist

Dr Koh Hong Yi

MBBS · MRCP (UK) · MMed (Internal Medicine) · FAMS (Dermatology)

Clinical interests include skin cancer, Mohs micrographic surgery, dermatologic surgery and inflammatory skin conditions. His background includes specialist practice at Singapore General Hospital.

Read Dr Koh’s profile

Founder · Senior Consultant Dermatologist

Dr Tham Siew Nee

MBBS (Adelaide) · MMed (Internal Medicine) · FAMS (Dermatology)

Founded the clinic in 1996. Her interests include eczema, psoriasis, pigment disorders and phototherapy. She previously served as Deputy Medical Director of the National Skin Centre.

Read Dr Tham’s profile

Dr Koh joined the practice in 2020. The clinic adopted the TSN Dermatology name in 2022, continuing the practice established by Dr Tham.

Before your visit

Helpful answers.
A little clarity.

For individual appointment or payment questions, contact the clinic team.

Do I need a referral?

The clinic states that no referral is required. Call 6476 6821, WhatsApp the team or use the online enquiry form to request a consultation.

Who are the clinic’s dermatologists?

Dr Koh Hong Yi and Dr Tham Siew Nee are Senior Consultant Dermatologists at TSN Dermatology. Read their introductions and follow the links to their full profiles.

Is the clinic open during weekday lunchtime?

The published weekday hours are 8.30am–1pm and 2pm–5pm, with a break from 1pm–2pm. Saturday hours are 8.30am–1pm. The clinic is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Can I ask about insurance?

Some medically indicated procedures may be claimable. Ask the clinic and your insurer to confirm eligibility and policy requirements before treatment.

Does sending an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. The clinic team needs to confirm your appointment date and time. Contact the team if you have not received confirmation.
