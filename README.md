TSN Dermatology — Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview

TSN Dermatology Skin Specialist Clinic is a dermatology practice based at Gleneagles Medical Centre, Singapore, providing medical, surgical, and cosmetic dermatology services.

The clinic serves patients seeking professional care for skin, hair, and nail conditions, with specialist expertise in areas including eczema, psoriasis, skin cancer, and cosmetic dermatology.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the clinic and its two dermatologists clearly while improving mobile accessibility and making essential appointment and contact actions easier to use.

2. Main Improvements

Three key issues identified in the previous homepage were addressed:

1. Improved Mobile Appointment Button Readability

Previous issue: The appointment button in the mobile header used text as small as 10px, making it difficult to read and interact with.

Implemented fix:

Increased appointment button text to more readable sizes.
Added larger touch targets for easier mobile interaction.

Result: The appointment action is more accessible and easier to identify and use on mobile devices.

2. Both Dermatologists Displayed on the Homepage

Previous issue: The homepage initially displayed only one detailed doctor profile, requiring visitors to make another selection to view the other dermatologist.

Implemented fix:

Displayed Dr Tham Siew Nee and Dr Koh Hong Yi together.
Added introductions for both dermatologists.
Included links to their respective full profiles.

Result: Visitors can immediately see both specialists and access their individual profiles without additional selection.

3. Consolidated Mobile Contact Actions

Previous issue: The call action was located in the header, while WhatsApp was available through a separate floating control.

Implemented fix:

Added a persistent mobile contact bar.
Combined two clearly labelled actions: Call Clinic and WhatsApp.
Made both contact options accessible from the same location.

Result: Mobile visitors can quickly choose their preferred way to contact the clinic without searching across different interface elements.

3. Design Improvements

The homepage redesign introduces several focused improvements:

Visual presentation: Improved the visibility and presentation of important homepage elements, particularly appointment and contact actions.
Layout: Presented both dermatologists together in a more accessible homepage layout.
Mobile user experience: Improved button readability, touch targets, and access to essential contact options.
Navigation and accessibility: Reduced the need for additional selections to discover doctor information and consolidated mobile contact actions.
Consistency: Brought calling and WhatsApp actions together in a clearly labelled, persistent mobile interface.
Overall appearance: Created a clearer, more organised homepage experience focused on the clinic's dermatology services, specialists, and patient enquiries.
4. Summary

The redesigned TSN Dermatology homepage addresses three specific usability issues: small mobile appointment text, limited visibility of the two dermatologists, and separated mobile contact actions.

These changes make the homepage more accessible, improve the presentation of the clinic's specialists, and provide a more straightforward experience for visitors accessing the website on mobile devices.

The redesign retains its focus on communicating the clinic's professional dermatology services and helping prospective patients connect with the clinic.
